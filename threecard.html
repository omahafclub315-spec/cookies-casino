Rules & house edge

  • Make an Ante (and/or Pair Plus), see your 3 cards, then Play (bet = Ante) or Fold
  • Dealer needs Queen-high to qualify. No qualify: Ante pays 1:1, Play pushes
  • Qualifies: higher hand wins Ante and Play 1:1; ties push
  • Ranking: straight flush, trips, straight, flush, pair, high card
  • Ante bonus is paid whenever you play; Pair Plus pays on your hand alone
  • House edge: Ante/Play 3.37% per ante (Q-6-4 strategy), Pair Plus 2.32%
  • Limits: Ante $5–$1,000 · Pair Plus $1–$1,000
