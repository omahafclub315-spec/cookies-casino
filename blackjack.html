Rules & payouts

  • 6 decks, reshuffled when the cut card (75%) comes out
  • Blackjack pays 3:2 · wins pay 1:1
  • Dealer stands on soft 17 and peeks for blackjack
  • Insurance pays 2:1 (offered on an Ace)
  • Double on any two cards, also after splitting
  • Split up to 4 hands · split Aces get one card, no resplit
  • Late surrender: give up half your bet
  • Table limits $5 – $5,000
  • 💡 Hints and the Chart use the basic strategy for exactly these rules (6 decks, S17, DAS, late surrender)
  • House edge ≈ 0.34% with perfect basic strategy
