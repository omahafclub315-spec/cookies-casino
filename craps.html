Two crypto-random dice. Odds 3-4-5× (pass/come); lay up to 6× (don't). Place bets and hardways are off on the come-out roll; come odds are off on the come-out (returned if decided); lay odds always work. Place and hardway bets stay up after a win. The Pass Line and come bets on a number can't be taken down; everything else can (Remove). Payouts round down to the cent. Bets stay on the table if you leave and come back. Limits: line/come/place/field $5–$5,000, hardways and props $1–$1,000, $25,000 on the layout.
